## The reply

> I'm on vacation. I delegated your email to this auto-reply, which has just filed for vacation too.

98 characters. Why this one: a recursive delegation joke — the machine voice carries
the laugh (an agent that delegates even its own out-of-office reply, and the reply
itself then asks for vacation too). It reads as a real auto-reply someone would
receive, has a surprising turn rather than a mild pun, and steers clear of the usual
beach, inbox and back-soon cliches.
